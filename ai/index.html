Where it pays off

Sharpest ROI where an automation touches revenue or a salaried headcount.

Real estate

Leads go cold in minutes; agents drown in admin.
Lead-response agent — answers, qualifies, and books every inbound lead in under a minute, then follows up on schedule.
Goal: response time from hours to seconds; more leads worked with the same staff (see speed-to-lead data above).

Clinics & local services

Missed calls, no-shows, front desk underwater.
AI front desk — answers calls and messages 24/7, books and confirms appointments, sends reminders, escalates the rest to a human.
Goal: fewer missed enquiries and no-shows, measured against your current booking numbers.

E-commerce & DTC

Abandoned carts and a slow support queue.
Recovery + support agent — re-engages abandoned carts and resolves routine tickets, escalating edge cases with full context.
Goal: recover a measurable share of lost carts; deflect routine support — scoped to your data.

Agencies & ops teams

Manual reporting and reconciliation eat billable time.
Reporting agent — pulls from your tools, drafts the recurring report or reconciliation, and flags anomalies for review.
Goal: reclaim recurring hours each week from work nobody should do by hand.

Law & professional firms

Contract review and intake eat billable hours.
Clause-review + intake agent — a retrieval-grounded assistant that surfaces relevant clauses and structures new-client intake for a human to approve.
Higher-ticket, longer cycle — scoped carefully, human approval on every output.
