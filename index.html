Agent ecosystem architecture

A diagram of how work moves through the lab. It shows structure and direction of flow only; it carries no counts, metrics or live state. Bronze lines are delegation travelling outward from a parent. Slate lines are inbound polling arriving at the core. Sage lines are verified returns: QA passes and evidence coming back.

  • Orchestrator, at the centre. Routing and the QA gate. Sole writer of READY to IN PROGRESS, and the only hand that promotes work to DONE. It delegates to two loops.
    • Production loop, upper left. The kit production line. It receives delegation from the orchestrator and owns four stages.
      • Demand gate
      • Design
      • Asset delegation
      • QA return. This stage runs back into the orchestrator on a distinct return path, drawn separately from every outbound line.
    • Marketing loop, upper right. Publishing and distribution. It receives delegation from the orchestrator and owns three stages.
      • Publish
      • Outreach
      • Copy
  • Inbound pollers, lower left. Five pollers, each pointing into the orchestrator.
    • Steering
    • Heavy
    • Mid
    • Fast
    • Judgment
  • Terminal operators, a wide arc across the lower field. Sixteen small execution nodes, placed by a golden-angle distribution rather than at random. Each operator has three kinds of connection:
    • Delegation down: operators on the left half of the arc receive work from the production loop, operators on the right half from the marketing loop.
    • Lateral chatter: each operator links to its neighbour along the arc, with longer skip links every fifth node.
    • Evidence return: every operator returns evidence to the orchestrator.
