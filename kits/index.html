Kits

Systems you can install, not courses you have to finish

Each kit is a working pipeline with the discipline built in as code. Scripts that fail closed, so the process cannot advance when a step has been skipped. You buy it once, run it yourself, and it costs nothing to keep.

These came out of my own work. I needed a repeatable way to do something, built the machinery, then packaged the machinery. That is the whole origin story and it is worth knowing, because it means each one is shaped by what actually goes wrong rather than by what makes a tidy curriculum.

  • 69 USD · 5 human steps · 75 min setup Autonomous Kindle Publishing Pipeline Seven gates as running code. Demand validated against real Amazon searches before a word is written, one section at a time with four named review checks, and a cover judged at the size buyers actually decide at.
  • 29 USD · 4 human steps · 20 min setup MCP Reliability Audit Kit The repeatable version of an MCP security audit. Two skills, a weekly cadence, a client-ready report template, and the authorisation discipline it refuses to let you skip.
Before you buy anything here

Nobody has bought either kit yet and there is no case study. I am not going to invent a testimonial or a customer count to make this page look busier than it is. What I can offer instead is founding terms: buy one and I will work through your first run with you, by email.

Each kit page states its own honest limitations near the top rather than in the small print, including the parts that could break and the steps a person still has to do by hand. If you read one and it looks thinner than you hoped, that is the page doing its job.
