Privacy Policy

This site has no analytics and sets no cookies of its own. Here is the complete list of what happens to your data, including the parts most policies skip.

Last updated 4 August 2026

This site has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising tags and sets no cookies of its own. I checked that against the live pages rather than assuming it. What follows is the short list of what actually happens to your data.

What I collect, and only when you act

WhatWhenWhy, and who else sees it
Your email addressYou submit the waitlist form on a kit pageTo tell you when that kit is available. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, which forwards it to my inbox. Nothing else is asked for and there is no newsletter.
Name, email, billing country, payment detailsYou buy somethingCollected and held by Paddle as merchant of record, inside their checkout. I receive your email address and country so I can deliver the file and account for the sale. I never receive your card number.
Your email address, againImmediately after a purchasePassed to Resend, the service that sends your download email. Nothing else travels with it.
An email you send meYou email meIt sits in my Gmail inbox, like any other email.

Third parties that see something just because you loaded a page

Being straight about this, since most privacy policies quietly skip it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site, so GitHub receives your IP address as part of serving it.
  • Google Fonts serves the two typefaces on every page, which means Google receives your IP address when the font files load.
  • Paddle loads its checkout script on kit pages only. Paddle sets its own cookies when you open a checkout, governed by their privacy notice rather than mine.

That is the complete list. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no session recording, no heatmap tool, no A/B testing script and no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.

How long things are kept

Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require, which is a Paddle obligation as much as mine. Waitlist emails are kept until the kit launches and I have told you, then deleted. Support emails stay in my inbox unless you ask me to delete them.

What I will never do

Sell your data. Rent it. Pass it to an advertiser. Add you to a mailing list you did not ask for. Use your email for anything other than the specific thing you gave it to me for.

Your rights

Email m.ashrey122@gmail.com and ask me to show you what I hold, correct it, or delete it, and I will do it. If you are in the UK, EU or another jurisdiction with data protection law, you have those rights formally and I am not going to make you cite the statute to exercise them. For data Paddle holds as merchant of record, their privacy notice and their processes apply, and I will point you to the right place if you ask.

Children

These products are sold to adults. I do not knowingly collect anything from anyone under 16.

Changes

If I add anything that collects data, this page changes in the same update. The date at the top is the last time that happened.
