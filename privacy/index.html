Privacy Policy

This site has no analytics and sets no cookies of its own. Here is the complete list of what happens to your data, including the parts most policies skip.

Last updated 9 August 2026

This site has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising tags and sets no cookies of its own. That was checked against the live pages rather than assumed. What follows is the short list of what actually happens to your data.

What gets collected, and only when you act

Collection ledger Complete list
What When Why, and who else sees it
Your email address You submit the waitlist form on a kit page To tell you when that kit is available. The form is delivered by Web3Forms, which forwards it to the lab inbox. Nothing else is asked for and there is no newsletter.
Name, email, billing country, payment details You buy something Collected and held by Paddle as merchant of record, inside their checkout. The lab receives your email address and country in order to deliver the file and account for the sale. Your card number is never received here.
Your email address, again Immediately after a purchase Passed to Resend, the service that sends your download email. Nothing else travels with it.
An email you send You email the lab It sits in a Gmail inbox, like any other email.

Third parties that see something just because you loaded a page

Being straight about this, since most privacy policies quietly skip it:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site, so GitHub receives your IP address as part of serving it.
  • Google Fonts serves the three typefaces on every page, which means Google receives your IP address when the font files load.
  • Cloudflare serves the three.js library used by the graphic on the home page, from its public CDN, so it receives your IP address on that page only.
  • Paddle loads its checkout script on kit pages only. Paddle sets its own cookies when you open a checkout, governed by their privacy notice rather than this one.

That is the complete list. There is no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no session recording, no heatmap tool, no A/B testing script and no consent banner, because there is nothing to consent to.

How long things are kept

Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require, which is a Paddle obligation as much as ours. Waitlist emails are kept until the kit launches and you have been told, then deleted. Support emails stay in the inbox unless you ask for deletion.

What will never happen

Selling your data. Renting it. Passing it to an advertiser. Adding you to a mailing list you did not ask for. Using your email for anything other than the specific thing you gave it for.

Your rights

Email m.ashrey122@gmail.com and ask to see what is held, correct it, or delete it, and it gets done. If you are in the UK, EU or another jurisdiction with data protection law, you have those rights formally and you will not be made to cite the statute to exercise them. For data Paddle holds as merchant of record, their privacy notice and their processes apply, and you will be pointed to the right place if you ask.

Children

These products are sold to adults. Nothing is knowingly collected from anyone under 16.

Changes

If anything is added that collects data, this page changes in the same update. The date at the top is the last time that happened.
