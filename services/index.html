Frequently asked

What happens right after I pay?

You get a reply within one business day. For the security audit and the content offer, that reply asks for what I need (repo access, or your topic and audience). For the automation audit, that reply is a link to book the 30 minute intake call. The clock on delivery starts once I have what I need from you, not the moment payment lands.

What's the refund policy?

If I can't deliver what's described above, you get a full refund, no argument. Once a report or article is delivered as scoped, the work is considered complete. If something in the deliverable is flat wrong, I'll fix it.

What do you need from me to get started?

Security audit: read access to the server's source (a repo link is enough) or the code itself. Automation audit: 30 minutes on a call and a plain description of the workflow you want looked at. Content: a topic or keyword, your intended audience, and any style guide or examples you already have.

Why are there no client logos or testimonials?

Because this is a new practice and I'm not going to invent them. The evidence right now is the open-source tools this audit is based on and the blog posts published daily, both public and both checkable. Real testimonials will get added, with permission, once real clients exist.
